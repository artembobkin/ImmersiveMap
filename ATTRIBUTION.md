# Attribution

**Keep the map data credit visible wherever the map is.**

With the built-in tiles the credit is **© OpenStreetMap**, linking to [openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The tiles are a planet build assembled from OpenStreetMap data, under ODbL. The engine shows that credit in a badge on the map by default, so a plain `ImmersiveMapView()` is compliant with no configuration.

The badge is restylable (size, position, text color) and can be hidden, but hiding it does not remove the obligation: the credit must still be visible on the map screen itself, at every zoom, on every screen that shows a map. A line only on an About or Settings page does not satisfy the license.

A tile source of your own carries whatever its data requires, which the engine cannot know: read your source's terms and declare the credit it asks for.

Crediting ImmersiveMap itself is not required.
