# Frame loop and render pipeline

The frame loop itself is described in the root CLAUDE.md (rendering is on-demand). What follows is the render pipeline, pass by pass.

`RenderFrameEngine` (`Render/RenderFrameEngine.swift`) runs per-frame stages: collectInput (camera/presentation/visible-tile resolution, `FrameContext`) → updateScene → prepareGPU → encodePasses → presentFrame. Work is organized as ~17 `RenderSubsystem`s, each in its feature's folder (the protocol and the registry in `Render/Core`), registered by `RenderGraphFactory`; `RenderPassGraph` groups `RenderLayer`s into up to six passes: `shadowMap` (flat-only depth pass from the directional light over building/model casters: ONE `depth16Unorm` map, not a cascade set, fitted by `ShadowFrameStateResolver` in `Shadows/` to a pose-invariant disc of `ShadowSettings.coverageCameraDistances` camera distances (default 3) at `mapResolution` (default 2048), so each caster draws once into a plain 2D attachment with no instancing, no `[[render_target_array_index]]` and no layered rendering, which is why the pass now runs on the iOS Simulator too; cached across frames by `ShadowMapReuseController` (the sun is static, so the pass re-runs only when the camera leaves the margined fit, the light/resolution changes, a new caster tile arrives, or scene models cast; other frames reuse the rendered map through matrices re-materialized for the current pan), gated by `ShadowPassGateResolver`, sampled by the building/model shaders and by the `groundShadowMask` pass. Sampling is one 3x3 Castaño tent (four hardware bilinear compares with computed weights, `shadowWindowVisibility` in `Render/Shaders/Shared/RenderUniforms.h`), read by every receiver alike (the ground through its mask, buildings, scene models), with no receiver-plane gradient; `ShadowSettings.softness` (1...2.5, default 1) scales the ramp by pushing the four taps out about the sample point, which is free and, being symmetric, lengthens the ramp without moving the shadow's edge: acne is prevented by the normal offset (`normalOffsetTexels` window texels, uncapped, since the window is sized to the camera distance) plus a constant `depthBias`, and walls too oblique for that are declared self-shadowed by the `kShadowGeometricCutoffStart/End` band on N·L), `groundShadowMask` (flat-only, right after the shadow map: one fullscreen triangle that intersects every pixel's view ray with the ground plane and samples the map there, into an `r8Unorm` texture at 0.4 of the drawable size that every blended ground layer of the world pass reads with one texture read instead of sampling the shadow map again per layer (no screen derivatives, so pixels above the horizon or beyond the shadow fade exit early); `Shadows/Shaders/GroundShadowMask.metal`, `GroundShadowMaskRenderSubsystem`, gated by the same `ShadowPassGateResolver`; the ground pipeline selects the mask read with a function constant), `buildingImage` (flat-only, translucently composited buildings: opaque offscreen render of buildings that `buildingExtrusion` composites over the map with a frame-resolved alpha, see `BuildingExtrusionPathResolver`), `world` (one sample per pixel; ground lines are antialiased analytically in the tile shaders: on the globe the starfield first, then the tiles' ground geometry projected onto the sphere in the vertex shader by `GlobeVectorSurfaceRenderSubsystem` / `Tile/Shaders/TileSphere.metal`, blending over the sky, neither testing nor writing depth (nothing writes surface depth on the sphere any more; the tiles' extent is the slot clip distances plus back-face culling), the morph is the sphere-to-plane unroll (`Globe/Shaders/GlobeUnroll.h`, mirrored by `Globe/GlobeUnrollMath.swift`), whose journey cut hides mid-morph travellers as a fifth clip distance; unlit on the sphere, through the unfurl and on the plane (no surface lighting, no earth scene, no horizon fog inside the tile shaders); the air around the surface's edge is the `horizon` layer that closes the world pass on both surfaces (`HorizonRenderSubsystem`, `Horizon/Shaders/Horizon.metal`, the edge and the per-frame schedule in `Horizon/`): a fullscreen analytic draw split by the depth buffer into a sky side over unpainted pixels and a ground side over painted ones, painting on the globe the optional atmosphere (`AtmosphereSettings`) plus the always-on limb feather that hides the mesh silhouette's staircase, on the flat map only the fog band into `mapClearColor` below the horizon line (nothing above it), and through the morph the handover of the first two into the last on the semantic transition between 0.5 and 0.9; the parser pre-splits coarse ground triangles on a grid, `Tile/Parse/GroundGeometrySubdivider.swift`, so their chords follow the sphere's curvature; a tile that has not arrived leaves its slot unpainted, the sky showing through, and cap (a constant style colour per pole, nothing baked: the north cap is the palette's open ocean, the south its polar ice, see `ImmersiveMapBaseColors`), and on the plane a tile-ownership stencil prepass first (`TileOwnershipRenderSubsystem`: one full-extent quad per unique source, no fragments, writing only the tile-priority stencil; it repeats at the start of the offscreen buildingImage pass), then the solid buildings (every one of them, whole and opaque at every size on screen: there is no level of detail and no translucent path, and the footprint radius baked per vertex, `ExtrudedVertexIn.footprintRadius`, is read by no shader) and then the flat surface, which depth-tests against them without writing so nothing under a building is shaded (`RenderPassGraph.worldLayerOrder`; composited buildings keep the ground first; the flat ground's opaque fills are followed by their fill outlines, the fills' ring edges drawn as one-pixel line primitives in the fill colour with alpha by distance to the edge, the Mapbox `fill-antialias` construction: `kTileFillOutline` in `Tile.metal`, the third class segment of the ground index buffer baked by the parser, at the fill's own rank depth under a lessEqual test so a fringe never covers a higher opaque layer, flat only; the rank depth rides in the vertex z for the target zoom's tiles and is written by the fragment stage as an exact constant (`kTileExactRankDepth`, `tileExactDepthFragmentShader`, `FlatMapSurfaceDrawer.usesExactRankDepth`) for every coarser source, because the clipper's float arithmetic at the near cut moves a large triangle's interpolated depth by thousands of rank steps and a coarse tile's base and landcover then swap from frame to frame; the road buckets of a flat-era tile are deferred ribbons: the parser keeps the centreline and a unit direction per vertex (`TileVertexIn.normal`, `ParseLine.parse(deferredExtrusion:)`), and the flat vertex shader extrudes each vertex by the style's width resolved in pixels at that vertex (`tileLineEdgePixels`, one pixel of feather past it), so a road is as wide on screen as its style says at every distance, a stroke's point floor holds to the horizon, and no ribbon is a sub-pixel sliver; the fragment reads that width from a flat interpolant and keeps the pixel-wide-with-alpha floor for anything narrower (`tileLineCoverage`); sphere-era tiles keep baked ribbons; the flat fills also carry the footprint fade, `tileFootprintFadeAmount` in `TileShading.h` mirrored by `GroundFootprintFade`: where a pixel's ground footprint in the source tile's units exceeds the band, the fill blends toward its style's far colour pair, `TilePolygonStyle.farColor`, and the outline fades with it), scene models, routes; no tile shader discards, and neither the ground/road tile shaders nor the world-pass buildings carry slot clips: which tile owns a pixel is decided by the tile-priority stencil (`TileSourceStencilPriority`, depth32Float_stencil8 attachment; finer sources write their mark, coarser substitutes fail the test, and the buildings test the prepass marks), with clip distances remaining only on the shadow-caster path (the shadow pass has no stencil) and the morph's unroll cut, so hidden surface removal stays on for the whole pass except the exact-depth draws of the coarse sources), `postProcessing` (FXAA), `overlay` (scene-model label-occlusion depth prepass, then labels/avatars/debug: labels rasterize at the far plane and depth-test against the prepass, so model silhouettes clip them). GPU frame overlap is bounded by `InFlightFramePool`; frame-slotted dynamic buffers avoid CPU/GPU write races. Immutable GPU state (pipeline states, text atlases, marker SDFs, cap geometry) is built once per process in `Render/Bootstrap/SharedRenderResources.swift` and reused by every map view and every settings-driven renderer rebuild, and dismantled host views are parked by `ImmersiveMapHostViewPool` for the next screen to adopt warm (`ViewReuseSettings`). Every frame, frame stage, render pass and tile stage is wrapped in an `os_signpost` interval (`Utils/MapSignposts.swift`, subsystem `ImmersiveMap`, categories `Render` and `Tiles`), so a slow frame is read in Instruments rather than guessed at. The same engine also renders offscreen (`render(offscreen:)`), which is what the video and still recorders drive from their own headless instance while the on-screen map keeps its state.
